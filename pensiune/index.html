Our story

Three generations of mountain hospitality

We built Vârful Lin with our own hands, beam by beam, on the land where our grandparents raised sheep. Today we welcome hikers, skiers and families who want a quiet place to slow down.

Every morning starts with fresh bread, cheese from local shepherds and jams we make every summer. Every evening ends by the fire.

Experiences

Something for every season

Ask us at breakfast and we'll plan your day: trails, guides, gear and the best places to eat nearby.
